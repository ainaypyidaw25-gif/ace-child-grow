# Product Overview

**ACE Child Grow — Every Child Can Grow.** Bilingual Myanmar–English early-
childhood (0–5) development support for parents and caregivers.

## What it helps parents do
Understand age-appropriate development; track milestones; recognise possible
concerns early (without diagnosis); learn safe home activities; understand
special needs without stigma; track growth and sleep; prepare reports for
professional visits; know when to observe, repeat a review, consult, or seek
urgent care; and access educational content offline.

## Core principles
Safety first (fixed rule-based urgent guidance, no AI); no diagnosis; parent-
friendly Myanmar language; strengths-based; private by default; clinically
reviewable content; offline-aware; low AI cost.

## Age groups (10)
Birth–2m, 3–4m, 5–6m, 7–9m, 10–12m, 13–18m, 19–24m, 2–3y, 3–4y, 4–5y.

## Development domains (10)
Gross Motor, Fine Motor, Speech & Language, Cognitive, Social & Emotional, Play,
Self-help, Growth & Nutrition, Sleep, Safety & Health.

## MVP success criteria
A parent can create an account, add children, get correct (corrected) age
guidance, complete/resume a milestone review, get a safe rule-based result,
trigger urgent guidance for skill loss/emergencies, follow age-appropriate
activities, read bilingual education & awareness content, record growth & sleep,
generate a Myanmar-compatible monthly PDF, use saved content offline, and
export/delete their data.

## Non-negotiable safety rules
Never infer autism %, disorder probability, IQ, "normal/abnormal child",
checklist diagnoses, unsupported outcomes, or fabricated contacts/percentiles.
Every result states it is not a diagnosis. Urgent logic never depends on AI.
