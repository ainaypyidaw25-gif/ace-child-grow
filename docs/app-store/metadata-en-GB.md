## Description

ACE Child Grow helps parents and caregivers support a child's early development
in Myanmar and English.

With ACE Child Grow, you can:

- create a private child profile using a nickname and birth date;
- use published age-based milestone checklists and save observations;
- explore simple, age-based play activities and save favourites;
- record growth and sleep;
- keep vaccination, health and medicine records;
- read published parent guides, lessons and stories; and
- export or delete your account data.

Published educational content appears only after the app's content-review
workflow marks it ready for parents.

ACE Child Grow is an educational and development-monitoring support tool. It
does not diagnose a condition, calculate disease probabilities, prescribe
treatment, or replace a qualified health professional. Seek professional or
emergency care whenever you are concerned about a child.

## Review notes draft

ACE Child Grow is designed for parents and caregivers, not for direct use by
children. This first iOS release is free. It contains no purchase flow, external
payment link, or paid-feature unlock.

Sign in with the dedicated review account provided in App Store Connect. The
account contains fictional child data only. Login uses the app's own email and
six-digit PIN. Google sign-in and staff/admin access are not present in the iOS
build.

Account deletion: Profile -> Privacy and account -> Delete account. The flow
requires confirmation, then deletes the account and associated child records.

Milestone results are educational, rule-based groupings and are not diagnoses or
probability scores. Safety guidance follows the app's clinically reviewed rules;
the final wording and behaviour require clinical-owner sign-off before release.
Growth entries are user records and do not claim to provide a clinical growth
assessment.

Reviewer credentials, contact details, and any required clinical methodology
references must be entered directly in App Store Connect, not committed here.
