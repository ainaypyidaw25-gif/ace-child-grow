## Safety and wording decisions required before approval

1. **Make reflux positioning conditional and protect sleep safety.** Replace the universal “Hold upright to burp after a feed” with clinician-final wording such as: “If your baby has reflux or seems uncomfortable, ask a health professional about feeding position and burping. For sleep, always place your baby flat on their back.” A safe-sleep source must be linked if the sleep sentence is retained.
2. **Use the source's exact age boundary for formula risk.** Change “babies under 2 months” to “babies younger than 2 months.” A reviewer must confirm how the birth–2-month UI handles a baby on the exact two-month boundary.
3. **Localize formula preparation, not just the noun “safe water.”** A Myanmar infant-feeding/public-health reviewer must specify the locally valid preparation method, water source, sanitation/sterilization term, burn prevention, storage/discard timing, and emergency-water advice. Do not combine CDC's ordinary-water and emergency-water pathways.
4. **Keep green vomit and forceful vomiting separate.** Green/bilious vomit remains an emergency-now instruction. Forceful/projectile vomiting remains urgent assessment even if it has not occurred after every feed. A clinician must decide whether Myanmar copy needs a parent-understandable colour qualifier without delaying care or confusing normal milk-coloured spit-up.
5. **Clarify dehydration/weak-feeding thresholds.** “Clear drop in wet nappies” is deliberately non-numeric outside the day-5 breastfeeding FAQ. A clinician must approve the urgent route for the baby's exact age and ensure the FAQ's six-nappy example is not applied to the first days or used alone.
6. **Do not turn three weeks into a wait-until date.** Preserve “most,” professional assessment, and the explicit instruction not to wait when feeding, hydration, alertness, or health is concerning. Recheck the NHS weight page on or after 2026-09-20 because its displayed review is due then.
7. **Resolve unsupported general advice.** Add an official source and precise wording for `parentTips[0]`, or remove it. Keep “look at and talk to your baby,” “quiet, comfortable spot,” and encouragement as non-medical supportive copy unless the reviewer requires separate sourcing.
8. **Native Myanmar review is a release blocker.** Clinically validate `အစိမ်းရောင် အန်ဖတ်`, `အားဖြင့် ပန်းထွက်အောင် အန်ခြင်း`, `ဆီးများများစိုသော အနှီး`, urgent versus emergency phrasing, “powdered formula is not sterile,” water-to-powder ratio, and the younger-than-two-month boundary. English approval cannot cover semantic or action-level equivalence.
