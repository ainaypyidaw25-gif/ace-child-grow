ACE Child Grow is a bilingual Myanmar–English child-development support app for parents and caregivers.

• Age-appropriate developmental information
• Practical, low-cost play and learning activities
• Growth, sleep, and developmental tracking
• Parent guides and clear safety reminders
• Summaries to support conversations with a child’s health professional
• Special-needs learning resources and a verified service directory

Children develop at different rates. ACE Child Grow provides general education and observation support. It does not diagnose a condition and does not replace advice from a qualified health professional. If you are concerned about a child, consult an appropriate health professional.
